# Get Started

Surogate is an agentic engine based on deep understanding designed to extract insights and provide accurate answers from complex and unstructured data

Its main features include:

* **Deep Document Understanding**: Surogate employs sophisticated techniques to parse and understand complex document formats, ensuring high-quality knowledge extraction.
* **Template-Based Chunking**: It offers customizable templates for intelligent data segmentation, enabling users to extract relevant snippets while maintaining flexibility.
* **Grounded Citations**: Surogate minimizes hallucinations by providing traceable citations, ensuring reliable and truthful outputs.
* **Compatibility with Heterogeneous Data Sources**: The engine supports a wide range of formats, including Word documents, PDFs, images, web pages, and structured databases, enhancing usability across domains.
* **Automated RAG Workflow**: Surogate streamlines the retrieval-augmented generation process with configurable large language models (LLMs), embedding models, multiple recall capabilities, and intuitive APIs for seamless integration.
* **Graph-Based Workflows**: It supports advanced graph-based workflows for efficient data processing beyond traditional methods.

These features make Surogate a powerful tool for businesses to extract actionable insights, automate question-answering tasks, and integrate AI-driven solutions into their workflows effectively.
