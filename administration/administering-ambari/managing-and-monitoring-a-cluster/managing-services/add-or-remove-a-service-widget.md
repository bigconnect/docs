# Add or remove a service widget

Use options on **Ambari Web &gt; Services &gt; Metrics** to manage the metrics visible for a specific service.

**About this task**  
The **Ambari Web &gt; Services &gt; Metrics** tab displays metrics information using multiple widgets. You can add and remove widgets from the **Metrics** tab. HDP core services currently support this functionality, including HDFS, Hive, HBase, and YARN services.![](https://docs.cloudera.com/HDPDocuments/Ambari-2.7.3.0/managing-and-monitoring-ambari/how-to/images/amb_service_summary_metrics.png)

Steps

1. On the **Metrics** tab, either click + to launch the Widget Browser, or click **Actions &gt; Browse Widgets**.The **Widget Browser** displays service widgets available to add to your **Services tab**, widgets already added to your dashboard, shared widgets, and widgets you have created. Widgets that are shared are identified by the **Shared** icon. ![](https://docs.cloudera.com/HDPDocuments/Ambari-2.7.3.0/managing-and-monitoring-ambari/how-to/images/amb_widget_browser.png) 
2. In the Widget Browser, click the Show only my widgets check box.Only the widgets you have created display on the Metrics tab. 
3. In the **Widget Browser**, click a green, ADDED checkmark for any widget that displays one. The widget disappears from the the **Metrics** tab and no longer shows a green, ADDED checkmark in the **Widget Browser**. 
4. In the **Widget Browser**, for any availble widget not already added to your **Metrics** tab, click **Add**. The widget appears on the the **Metrics** tab and shows a green, ADDED checkmark in the **Widget Browser**.

**After you finish**  
 To remove a widget from the **Metrics** tab, click the edit widget icon ![](https://docs.cloudera.com/HDPDocuments/Ambari-2.7.3.0/managing-and-monitoring-ambari/how-to/images/amb_edit_widget_icon.png), then click **Delete**.
