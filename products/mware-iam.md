# MWARE Identity & Access Management

As an enterprise's applications, services, and API adoptions grow, managing identities (such as employees, vendors, partners, and customers) across internal, shared, and SaaS services becomes a significant challenge. MWARE Identity & Access Management (IAM) is a product that can meet this challenge and also provide options to solve identity problems you may encounter in future.

The MWARE IAM product documentation has been moved to [https://iam-docs.m-ware.eu](https://iam-docs.m-ware.eu)
