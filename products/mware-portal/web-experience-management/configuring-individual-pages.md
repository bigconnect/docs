# Configuring Individual Pages

After you’ve configured your Page Set, you can reconfigure some options at the individual page level. When you decide to customize a single page, options that were not available when initially creating a page appear. You can customize a page by navigating to _Pages_ under _Build_ menu and selecting _Options_ (![Options](https://liferay-support.zendesk.com/hc/article\_attachments/360030158711/icon-options.png)) → _Configure_ next to the page you want to edit from the navigation tree. Alternatively, you can click the _Configure_ icon on the top right of any page.
