# Enabling CAPTCHA on Form Submissions

[CAPTCHA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAPTCHA) prevents a bot from submitting forms. It’s often used in login apps, but you can also use it in the Forms app.

To enable CAPTCHA, click the form’s _Options_ button (![Options](https://liferay-support.zendesk.com/hc/article\_attachments/360030194852/icon-options.png)) and select _Settings_. Enable the _Require CAPTCHA_ setting, click _Done_, and save the form. That’s all there is to it!
