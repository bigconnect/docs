# Customizing Auto-Save Behavior

The _Forms_ application automatically saves the form creation progress as you [create a form](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/process-automation/forms/creating-and-managing-forms/creating-forms). By default, a form’s draft is auto-saved every minute.

To change the duration:

1. Navigate to the _Control Panel_ → _Configuration_ → _System Settings_.
2. Under _Content and Data_, click _Forms_.
3.  On the left menu, click _Forms_ under _Site Scope_.

    ![Customizing Forms System Settings](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/process-automation/forms/creating-and-managing-forms/customizing-auto-save-behavior/images/01.png)
4. Enter a different Autosave Interval value to increase the interval. To disable Autosave, enter 0.
5. Click _Save_.
