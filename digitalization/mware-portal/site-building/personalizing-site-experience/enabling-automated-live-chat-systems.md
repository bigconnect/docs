# Enabling Automated Live Chat Systems

MWARE Portal now offers integration with a number of live support chat platforms. Enabling this integration adds a chat window to sites on your portal instance and can be used to enable improved support and site experience for your users.

### Enabling Click To Chat <a href="#enabling-click-to-chat" id="enabling-click-to-chat"></a>

To enable Click To Chat go to _Instance Settings_.

1.  Go to the _Global Menu_ (![icon](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-applications-menu.png)) → _Control Panel_ → _Instance Settings_.

    ![Go to Instance Settings under Control Panel.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/personalizing-site-experience/enabling-automated-live-chat-systems/images/02.png)
2.  Under the _Content and Data_ section, click _Click To Chat_.

    ![You can see Click To Chat's icon under Content and Data.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/personalizing-site-experience/enabling-automated-live-chat-systems/images/03.png)
3.  To enable Click to Chat for your instance, switch the _Enable Click to Chat_ toggle.

    ![Toggle the switch on to enable Click to Chat.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/personalizing-site-experience/enabling-automated-live-chat-systems/images/04.png)
4.  Once enabled, you must select a Site Settings Strategy. Your Site Settings strategy determines how Click to Chat is enabled across sites that exist on a given portal instance.

    ![Choose your Site Settings Strategy option.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/personalizing-site-experience/enabling-automated-live-chat-systems/images/05.png)

    **Always Inherit**: Click To Chat is enabled for all sites on the instance and ignores configurations made in Site Settings.

    **Always Override**: Configuration for Click to Chat configured in Site Settings always takes priority over configurations made in Instance Settings.

    **Inherit or Override**: Sites inherit configuration from Instance Settings, unless configured otherwise in the site’s Site Settings.
5.  Select your chat provider and input the Account ID or token given to you by your Chat Provider.

    ![Select the chat provider you wish to use.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/personalizing-site-experience/enabling-automated-live-chat-systems/images/06.png)
6. (Optional) Click the _Guest Users Allowed_ toggle to configure whether you want guest users (unauthenticated users) to have access to the Click to Chat feature for your sites.
7. Click _Save_.

Click To Chat integration with your live chat provider is now configured.
