# Creating Teams for Sites

If you have an _ad hoc_ group of users who perform the same set of tasks in a site (moderating a site’s wiki content, managing message boards threads, writing blogs, editing a specific page in the site, etc.), you can organize them into site teams, and then assign the team permissions for various site-specific functions. Site teams are the preferred method for administering permissions within a single site. The permissions assigned to a site team only apply to that site.

NOTE

To create and apply permissions for a group of users to use across multiple sites or organizations in your Liferay portal instance, consider aggregating the users into a [user group](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/users-and-permissions/user-groups/creating-and-managing-user-groups) and assigning the user group permissions via [roles](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/users-and-permissions/roles-and-permissions/understanding-roles-and-permissions).

The steps below cover these topics:

* [Adding a Team to a Site](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites#adding-a-team-to-a-site)
* [Adding Members to a Team](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites#adding-members-to-a-team)
* [Managing Team Permissions](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites#managing-team-permissions)
* [Managing Team App Permissions](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites#managing-team-app-permissions)

### [Adding a Team to a Site](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites#adding-a-team-to-a-site) <a href="#adding-a-team-to-a-site" id="adding-a-team-to-a-site"></a>

1. Open the _Product Menu_ (![Product Menu](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-product-menu.png)), then click the compass icon (![Compass Icon](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-compass.png)) → Select a site.
2.  Select _People_ → _Teams_.

    ![Create teams through the People menu under the Product Menu.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites/images/01.png)

    NOTE

    You can configure other site membership groupings, such as Users, Organizations, and User Groups in the Memberships app, which is also under People. See [Changing Site Membership Type](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/site-settings/site-users/changing-site-membership-type) for more information on how site memberships work.
3. Click _Add_ (![Add Button](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-add.png)).
4. Enter a name and a description and click _Save_. Your new team is displayed in the list.

![Creating teams within your site can foster teamwork and collaboration, as team permissions enable team members to access the same resources and perform the same types of tasks.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites/images/02.png)

### [Adding Members to a Team](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites#adding-members-to-a-team) <a href="#adding-members-to-a-team" id="adding-members-to-a-team"></a>

1. Click the team name link.
2. Click _Add_ (![Add Button](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-add.png)).
3. Select the users to add to the team and click _Add_.

### [Managing Team Permissions](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites#managing-team-permissions) <a href="#managing-team-permissions" id="managing-team-permissions"></a>

1.  Click _Actions_ (![Actions](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-actions.png)) next to the team and select _Permissions_.

    NOTE

    Setting permissions for the team assigns those permissions to all the team’s members. Only administrators who can edit/manage the team can manage team permissions.
2. Check/uncheck the permissions and click _Save_.

### [Managing Team App Permissions](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites#managing-team-app-permissions) <a href="#managing-team-app-permissions" id="managing-team-app-permissions"></a>

You can also manage team permissions from an app.

1. Open the _Product Menu_ (![Product Menu](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-product-menu.png)) and select the app under the site’s menu (message boards for example).
2. Open the _Options_ (![Options](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-options.png)) menu in the top right of the screen and select the permissions-related option.
3. Find the team in the role column and select the appropriate permissions.

![The Lunar Resort message board moderators site team has unlimited permissions on the message boards application.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/sites/site-membership/creating-teams-for-sites/images/03.png)
