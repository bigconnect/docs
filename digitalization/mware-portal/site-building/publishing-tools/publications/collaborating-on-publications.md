# Collaborating on Publications

By default, a publication can only be accessed by its creator. However, publication creators can invite other users to view, edit, and publish their publications. Users can be added to any ongoing, scheduled, and published publications.

Once users are invited to a publication, you can use the integrated comments feature to facilitate collaboration between contributors.

### [Inviting Users to a Publication](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications#inviting-users-to-a-publication) <a href="#inviting-users-to-a-publication" id="inviting-users-to-a-publication"></a>

Follow these steps to invite user to collaborate on a publication.

1. Open the _Global Menu_ (![Global Menu](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-applications-menu.png)), click the _Applications_ tab, and go to _Publications_.
2.  Click the _Actions_ button (![Actions Button](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-actions.png)) for the desired publication and select _Invite Users_.

    ![Click the publication's Actions button and select Invite Users.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications/images/01.png)

    Alternatively, begin reviewing the publication’s changes and click _Invite Users_ (![Invite Users](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-plus.png)).

    ![In publication Changes page, click the Invite Users button.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications/images/02.png)

    NOTE

    This is the only way to invite users to a published publication.
3.  Use the drop-down menu to grant _view_, _edit_, _publish_, and _invite_ permissions.

    ![Use the drop-down menu to determine the users permissions for the publication.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications/images/03.png)
4.  Search for and select the desired _users_.

    Each user is assigned the selected permissions, though you can edit their permissions before and after sending invitations.

    ![Search for and select the desired users.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications/images/04.png)
5. Click _Send_ to add the users to the publication.

Invited users are notified and can begin collaborating on the publication.

### [Commenting on a Publication](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications#commenting-on-a-publication) <a href="#commenting-on-a-publication" id="commenting-on-a-publication"></a>

Collaborating users can add comments to a publication via its Changes page. Each comment is scoped to the publication in which they’re made.

1. Open the _Global Menu_ ( ![Global Menu](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-applications-menu.png) ), click the _Applications_ tab, and go to _Publications_.
2. Click the desired _publication_.
3. Click the _Comments_ button ( ![Comments Button](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-comments-w.png) ).
4.  In the chat window, type your comment, and click _Reply_.

    ![Click the Comments button, enter your comment, and click Reply.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications/images/05.png)

All collaborating users are notified of your comment and can reply as well.

### [Removing Users from a Publication](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications#removing-users-from-a-publication) <a href="#removing-users-from-a-publication" id="removing-users-from-a-publication"></a>

Follow these steps to remove a collaborator from a publication.

1. Open the _Invite User_ modal window for the desired publication.
2.  Click the drop-down menu for the desired user, and select _Remove_.

    ![Open the permissions drop-down menu and select Remove.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications/images/06.png)
3. Click _Save_.

### [Sharing Publications Via Link](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications#sharing-publications-via-link) <a href="#sharing-publications-via-link" id="sharing-publications-via-link"></a>

[Beta Feature](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/system-administration/configuring-liferay/feature-flags#beta-feature-flags)

Liferay 7.4 2023.Q3+/GA92+

If you need to share publication changes with people who are not site users, you can enable link sharing for the publication.

1.  Click _Actions_ (![Actions Button](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-actions.png)) for the desired publication, select _Invite Users_, and go to the _Share Link_ tab.

    ![Select Invite Users and go to the Share Link tab.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications/images/07.png)

    Alternatively, begin reviewing the publication’s changes and click _Share_ (![Share Button](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-link.png)).

    ![Click the Share button.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications/images/08.png)
2. Toggle the switch to enable sharing.
3.  Copy the link.

    ![Enable sharing and copy the link.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/site-building/publishing-tools/publications/collaborating-on-publications/images/09.png)

Anyone with the link can view the publication’s changes.
