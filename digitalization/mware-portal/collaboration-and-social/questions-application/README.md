# Questions Application

The Questions app is a new UI of the Message Boards application, focused on presenting a question and answer format. The Questions app allows the creation of,

**Topics:** Themes where questions are categorized **Questions:** A question inside the app **Answers:** An answer to a specific question

When questions are created, Tags can also be used to help others find relevant content.

### [Adding a Questions App to a Site](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app#adding-a-questions-app-to-a-site) <a href="#adding-a-questions-app-to-a-site" id="adding-a-questions-app-to-a-site"></a>

To add the Questions app to a Site,

1. Navigate to the site page where the app is desired.
2. Click on the Add icon (![Add icon](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-add-app.png)) at the top right of the page.
3.  Locate the app under the Collaboration section of widgets. Drag the app to the page.

    ![Locate the Questions app listed under the Collaboration section.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/14.png)

After adding the Questions app to a Site, topics can be created and users can begin asking and answering questions.

### [Creating Topics](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app#creating-topics) <a href="#creating-topics" id="creating-topics"></a>

1.  Click the _New Topic_ button.

    ![Click on the new topic button to create a new topic.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/01.png)
2.  Give your new topic a title and description and click the _Create_ button.

    ![Type a title and description for this new topic.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/02.png)

    You can create as many different topics as you like.

### [Asking Questions](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app#asking-questions) <a href="#asking-questions" id="asking-questions"></a>

1.  Once you’ve created a topic, you can begin asking questions. Click on the _Ask Question_ button to create a new question.

    ![Click on the Ask Question button to create a new question.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/03.png)
2.  A new window appears for your question. Fill in the title and body. Add optional tags to your question.

    ![Fill in the form with a title and body.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/04.png)

    To finish, click the _Post Your Question_ button.

### [Answering Questions](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app#answering-questions) <a href="#answering-questions" id="answering-questions"></a>

1. Users can view existing questions and give their replies. To give an answer, click on an existing question.
2.  Type your answer into the Your Answer box and click the _Post Answer_ button.

    ![Type your answer and click the Post Answer button](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/05.png)
3.  The answer is visible to everyone who views the question.

    ![The answers are visible below the question.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/06.png)

#### [Managing Answers](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app#managing-answers) <a href="#managing-answers" id="managing-answers"></a>

Once a question has been answered, those answers can be managed in the following ways:

**Rating:** You can give the answer a [rating](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/social-tools/user-guide/using-the-ratings-system) using the up or down arrows.

**Sort:** You can use the tabs to sort answers by Active, Oldest, or Votes.

**Reply:** Other users can reply to a given answer.

**Delete:** Users can delete their answers.

**Mark as answer:** The user who asked the question can mark an answer as the correct or most helpful response.

![Click on mark as answer to mark a response as the correct answer.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/07.png)

**Edit:** Users can edit their answers.

### [Searching Questions](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app#searching-questions) <a href="#searching-questions" id="searching-questions"></a>

There are a few different ways to search for questions:

![There are additional features for searching for questions.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/08.png)

**Filter By:** Filter the different questions based on criteria such as date and most votes.

**Search:** Search through different questions by typing keywords into the search bar.

**Tags:** Filter questions based on tags. Click the _Tags_ tab to see the available tags and their associated questions.

![Click on the tags tab to see all tags.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/09.png)

### [Subscriptions to Topics and Questions](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app#subscriptions-to-topics-and-questions) <a href="#subscriptions-to-topics-and-questions" id="subscriptions-to-topics-and-questions"></a>

You can subscribe to both topics and questions to receive in-site and email notifications for updates.

To subscribe to a topic, click the _Subscribe_ button below the topic title bar.

![Click on the subscribe button to subscribe to a topic.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/10.png)

To subscribe to a question, click the subscribe bell button next to the question. When you ask a question, you’re automatically subscribed to it.

![Click on the subscribe bell button to subscribe to a question](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/11.png)

To view your subscriptions, click on the _My Subscriptions_ tab. A list of all the topics and questions you are subscribed to appears.

![Click on the My Subscriptions tab to see all the things you are subscribed to.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/12.png)

### [My Activity](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app#my-activity) <a href="#my-activity" id="my-activity"></a>

You can track your use of the Questions app by clicking on the _My Activity_ tab.

![Click on the My Activity tab to track their use of the Questions app.](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/questions-application/using-the-questions-app/images/13.png)

Here is displayed your ranking, the number of questions they have asked, and a list of each of these questions.
