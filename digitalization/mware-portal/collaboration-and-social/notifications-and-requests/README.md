# Notifications and Requests

Users receive notifications and requests if they have subscribed to a blog or message board or are [mentioned](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/mentioning-users) in a post. They may also be notified if someone sent them a private message, invitation to join a site, or an event reminder. Lastly, users are notified to review content and asset creation when those activities are used with Liferay’s built-in [Workflow](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/process-automation/workflow/introduction-to-workflow) capabilities.

To access notifications and requests:

1.  Click your avatar and select _Notifications_.

    ![User Avatar and notifications](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests/images/01.png)
2.  The _Notifications List_ tab shows all unread notifications. Here, the user was mentioned in a Message Boards comment.

    ![User Avatar and notifications](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests/images/02.png)

### [Managing Notifications](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests#managing-notifications) <a href="#managing-notifications" id="managing-notifications"></a>

To manage and sort accumulated notifications:

1. Click the _Filter and Order_ dropdown menu.
2. Choose from:
   * **All:** The default option. Displays both read and unread notifications.
   * **Unread:** Displays notifications that haven’t been marked as read. Unread notifications are indicated with a blue border on the left-hand side of the notification.
   * **Read:** Displays notifications that have been marked as read.
   *   **Date:** Order notifications by date.

       ![Filter and Order menu](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests/images/03.png)

By default, notifications are listed by date in descending order. To sort notifications by ascending order, click the up/down arrow icon in the management bar.

To change your notification’s status:

1.  Click the _Actions_ menu (![Actions](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/images/icon-actions.png)) next to the notification.

    ![Notification status](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests/images/04.png)
2. Click _Mark as Read_ or _Mark as Unread_ to keep the notification fresh.
3. Alternately, click _Delete_ to remove the notification completely.

#### [Managing Multiple Notifications](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests#managing-multiple-notifications) <a href="#managing-multiple-notifications" id="managing-multiple-notifications"></a>

You can also manage multiple notifications at once:

1. Select the checkbox next to the _Filter and Order_ dropdown menu. This selects all the notification messages.
2.  Several icons appear:

    * **Mark as Read** (![Open Envelope](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests/images/icon-envelope-open.png))
    * **Mark as Unread** (![Closed Envelope](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests/images/icon-envelope-closed.png))
    * **Delete** (![Delete Button](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests/images/icon-delete.png))

    ![Managing Multiple Notification status](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests/images/05.png)

### [Managing Requests to Join a Site](https://learn.liferay.com/web/guest/w/dxp/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests#managing-requests-to-join-a-site) <a href="#managing-requests-to-join-a-site" id="managing-requests-to-join-a-site"></a>

When a user gets a request to join a site, it appears in the _Requests List_ tab. Users have the option to click _Confirm_ to join the site or click _Ignore_ to decline.

![Managing Multiple Notification status](https://resources.learn.liferay.com/images/dxp/latest/en/collaboration-and-social/notifications-and-requests/user-guide/managing-notifications-and-requests/images/06.png)
