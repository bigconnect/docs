# Duplicating Forms and Form Fields

Repetitive tasks are error prone. Instead of duplicating effort, duplicate forms and form fields.

To duplicate a form,

1. Go to _Site Administration_ (your Site’s menu) → _Content & Data_ → _Forms_.
2. Click the _Actions_ button (![Actions](https://liferay-support.zendesk.com/hc/article\_attachments/360030194512/icon-actions.png)) for the form to duplicate.
3. Click _Duplicate_.

![Figure 1: The Duplicate option works the same for forms and form fields.](https://liferay-support.zendesk.com/hc/article\_attachments/360030163091/forms-duplicate-form.png)

The form is duplicated and automatically named _Copy of \[Original Form Name]_. Once duplicated, you can edit the form however you want. When you duplicate a form, all configurations and form rules are duplicated as well.

To duplicate a form field,

1. Go to _Site Administration_ (your Site’s menu) → _Content & Data_ → _Forms_ and open or add a form.
2. In the Builder view, hover over the form field to duplicate and click the _Copy_ icon (![Copy](https://liferay-support.zendesk.com/hc/article\_attachments/360030163111/icon-copy.png)).

![Figure 2: You can duplicate form fields.](https://liferay-support.zendesk.com/hc/article\_attachments/360030163071/forms-duplicate-form-field.png)

The field is duplicated and labeled _Copy of \[Original Field Label]_. All the form’s properties, including its data provider configurations, are copied as well.
