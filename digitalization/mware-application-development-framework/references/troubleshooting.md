# Troubleshooting

If you are having some issues with your installation, there are a few things you can try out before raising an issue or discussion.

#### Make Sure It Is Up to Date

Running on the latest version of the platform ensures you have all the latest bug fixes, features, and much more. You can check which version  you are on from the Portal "Updates" section.

#### Clear Your Browser Cache and Cookies

Sometimes clearing your browser cookies and cache for your domain will solve certain issues, especially around authentication or user-based problems.
