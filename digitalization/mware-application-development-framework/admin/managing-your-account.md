# Managing your account

Whether you have a cloud or self-hosted account, you will need to log-in to the **online** account portal&#x20;

### Migrate between environments

> ### 📘
>
> If you are a **customer**, please contact our support and we will assist with the environment migration.

Before doing the migration, make sure you do any necessary Backups or App exports.

### Self-host account deletion

1. Log-in to the account portal online with your self-hosted account email address.
2.  Click the `Account` navigation item in your navigation bar.\


    <figure><img src="https://files.readme.io/2bbcd67-Screenshot_2023-11-10_at_12.47.53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3.  Click the `Deleted Account` button which is red and go through the confirmation window. (Your account will successfully be deleted. **This is permanent and irreversible**.)\


    <figure><img src="https://files.readme.io/730239e-Screenshot_2023-11-10_at_12.51.06.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
