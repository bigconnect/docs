# Theming your app

To streamline the design process, a Builder can update app-level theme settings. Updating these settings will update the style of all the screens within your app. Builder users can quickly update the theme of your app by selecting the screen in the component tree, and selecting the Theme tab in the settings panel.

### Theme

Changing the theme will change the color palette your app uses. This is a great feature and allows you to update the core look of your app with just one click.

The default theme is _Light_, however there are another themes of varying brightness to choose from, including _Nord_ and _Midnight_.

### Accent color + accent hover color

**Accent color**\
The accent color represents your brand's primary color. Changing this setting will change the color of your action button, links, input highlights, and more.

**Accent hover color**\
The accent hover color is the color of the element after a user has hovered their mouse over it.
