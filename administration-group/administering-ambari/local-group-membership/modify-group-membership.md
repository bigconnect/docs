# Modify group membership

Use **Admin/Groups** to add local users to an existing group.

1. On the **Ambari Admin** page, browse to **Users &gt; Groups**.
2. For a group name, under **Actions**, click **edit**.
3. In **Groups/\[group name\]**, use the **Local Members** control to modify group membership.
   1. Click in the **Local Members** text area.
   2. In the New control, type a new local user name,
   3. Click the **x** next to the name of a user to remove that user.
   4. To save your changes, click the check mark.
   5. To discard your changes, click **x**.
