# Embedding

### [Introduction](embedding-introduction.md) <a href="#introduction" id="introduction"></a>

What is embedding, and how does it work?

### [Interactive embedding](interactive-embedding.md) <a href="#interactive-embedding" id="interactive-embedding"></a>

The solution to self-service customer analytics: embed the full Answers app in your app. Interactive embedding integrates with your data permissions to let people slice and dice data on their own using Answers’s query builder.

### [Interactive embedding quick start](interactive-embedding-quick-start.md) <a href="#interactive-embedding-quick-start" id="interactive-embedding-quick-start"></a>

You’ll embed the full Answers application in your app. Once logged in, people can view a Answers dashboard in your web app, and be able to use the full Answers application to explore their data, and only their data.

### [Static embedding](static-embedding.md) <a href="#static-embedding" id="static-embedding"></a>

Also known as Signed Embedding, Static embedding offers drill-through with custom destinations, so you can define what happens when people click on a chart, like sending people to another chart or URL–all while securing the underlying data.

### [Parameters for signed embeds](parameters-for-static-embeds.md) <a href="#parameters-for-signed-embeds" id="parameters-for-signed-embeds"></a>

You can pass parameters between Answers and your website via the embedding URL to specify how Answers items should look and behave inside the iframe on your website.

### Public embeds <a href="#public-embeds" id="public-embeds"></a>

You can also create unsecured public links or embeds of questions and dashboards.
